Architecture and stories

The nine playable landmark miniatures are original artistic interpretations. They simplify architecture and proportions; they are not surveyed replicas. Puzzle sections are shape-matching activities, not historical construction sequences. Historical and measurement sources are linked beside each story in the app. Reference photographs are not redistributed.

The landscapes, gardens, neighboring buildings, canal, tram, boat and lighting arrangements are fictional designs. No endorsement by monument authorities or other credited creators is implied.

Geography

  • Manhattan shoreline: NYC Department of City Planning, Borough Boundaries, release 26b, downloaded 9 September 2026. Shoreline rings are simplified and projected into local metres, then uniformly converted at 0.07 render units per metre. Original data and processing evidence are retained in the project.
  • World land: Natural Earth, public-domain 1:110m land polygons. Landmark anchors use approximate longitude/latitude positions. The islands move apart to avoid overlaps, so their spacing is schematic and does not represent geographic distances.
