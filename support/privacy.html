Apple release candidate 0.2.0 · Updated 19 September 2026

This policy describes the Impossible City Apple app, provided by Parashar Shah. Contact GitHub support with privacy questions. GitHub issues are public; do not post personal information.

Playing the game

The app does not require an account and has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. It does not send your city name, layout, assembly progress, favorites or saved viewpoints to us. That information is stored locally in the app’s WebKit storage. Game assets and stories are included with the app.

There is no cloud-save service. Optional Pro purchases are processed by Apple; the app checks Apple-verified purchase information on your device to grant or restore access. We do not receive your card details or operate a purchase-account server. Apple handles payment and related store records under its own policies. Native app saves and website saves are separate. We cannot recover a city from our servers because we do not receive it. Removing the iPad or iPhone app can remove its local saves. On Mac, removing the app alone may leave its data container; contact support for help removing local data without affecting other apps. Device backups are managed by your operating-system settings.

TestFlight and Apple diagnostics

If you install through TestFlight, Apple processes testing information and may share beta usage, crash reports, device information and feedback with the developer. This is separate from the app’s own local storage. Review Apple’s TestFlight privacy information for the details and available choices.

Feedback you choose to send

If you open a support issue on GitHub, your issue text and attachments are public. We use the information you provide to investigate and respond. GitHub handles this information under its own policies and retention rules. Do not include personal information, payment receipts, passwords, or other sensitive details. Apple-managed feedback remains subject to Apple’s policies.

Links and this support site

Historical sources open external websites, which have their own privacy policies. This support site contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or cookies set by our code. Its hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect the site.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app adds features that change how information is handled. Accounts, cloud saves or additional data collection would need updated disclosures before they are introduced.
