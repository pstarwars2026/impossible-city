Download on the App Store. The iPhone/iPad listing is live. The approved native Mac version was released September 26, 2026; storefront availability may take up to 24 hours.

What can I build?

Build the Taj Mahal, Eiffel Tower and Sydney Opera House in three relaxed assembly activities. Name your city, arrange its landmarks and explore their stories. Pro — Edition One adds six more assembly puzzles: the Leaning Tower of Pisa, Elizabeth Tower, Temple of Heaven, Itsukushima Torii, Brandenburg Gate and Space Needle. Each includes sourced stories and close-up views. The three starter puzzles stay free.

Purchases and restoration

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. Check the app for the price in your region. Use Restore Purchases in the Pro screen with the same Apple Account to recover access on supported Apple devices. Future editions are separate; Edition One includes the six landmarks listed above. Web and Android purchase sharing is not available.

Where is my progress saved?

Assembly progress stays on your device. Use Save in My City to keep your current city and viewpoint, then Load to restore it. Saves do not sync between devices or the website. Removing the iPad or iPhone app can remove its saves, so please contact us before reinstalling to troubleshoot.

Something is not working

If the game cannot finish opening, use Try again. For a repeated problem, open a GitHub issue with your device model, operating-system version and what happened just before it. A screenshot is helpful if you are comfortable sharing one. Please leave out passwords, payment information and other private details.

Tell us what you think

Which landmark did you enjoy building? Was anything confusing? What would make you come back? You can open a GitHub issue or, if you installed through TestFlight, use its Send Beta Feedback option.

Devices and connectivity

The app targets iOS and iPadOS 26 or later, and native macOS 26 or later. The game content is packaged with the app. Opening historical sources and posting feedback requires an internet connection.

Credits and sources

Historical and measurement sources are linked from the stories in the app. The miniatures and assembly sections are artistic interpretations, not surveyed replicas or historical construction sequences.
