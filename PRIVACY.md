# Impossible City privacy

Updated September 20, 2026. This policy describes the submitted Apple app, provided by Parashar Shah.

## Your city stays on your device

Impossible City has no game account, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. City names, layouts, assembly progress, favorites and saved viewpoints stay in the app's local WebKit storage. The app does not send them to us. Game assets and stories are bundled with the app.

There is no cloud-save service. App and website saves are separate. We cannot recover a city from a server because we do not receive it. Removing the iPhone/iPad app can remove its saves. On Mac, removing the app alone may leave its data container; contact support for help removing local data. Operating-system settings control device backups.

## Purchases

Optional Pro purchases use Apple's App Store. The app checks Apple-verified transaction information on the device to grant or restore access. We do not receive card details or operate a purchase-account server. Apple handles billing and related store records under its own policies.

## Diagnostics and feedback

Apple may process diagnostics under your operating-system choices. If you install through TestFlight, Apple processes testing data and may share crash reports, device information and feedback with the developer. See [Apple's TestFlight privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/).

If you open a support issue on GitHub, your issue text and attachments are public. We use the information you provide to investigate and respond. GitHub handles this information under its own policies and retention rules. Do not include personal information, payment receipts, passwords, or private files. Apple-managed feedback remains subject to Apple's policies.

## External links and this page

Historical sources open external websites with their own privacy policies. This product page has no analytics scripts or forms added by us. GitHub hosts this page and processes ordinary connection information under its policies. GitHub issues are public; do not post personal information, receipts, passwords or private files.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use [GitHub support](https://github.com/pstarwars2026/impossible-city/issues/new/choose). We will update this policy before adding features that change how information is handled.
