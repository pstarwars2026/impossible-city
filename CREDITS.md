# Impossible City credits

The nine playable miniatures are original artistic interpretations with simplified proportions. They are not surveyed replicas, and the puzzle sections are not historical construction sequences. Historical and measurement sources appear beside stories in the app. Reference photographs are not redistributed. The surrounding city, gardens, water, transport and lighting are fictional.

Three.js uses the [MIT license](support/licenses/three-MIT.txt). Fraunces and Manrope use the SIL Open Font License: [Fraunces](support/licenses/Fraunces-OFL.txt), [Manrope](support/licenses/Manrope-OFL.txt).

Geographic references include [NYC Department of City Planning Borough Boundaries, release 26b](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Borough-Boundaries/gthc-hcne) and [Natural Earth public-domain land polygons](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/). Geography is simplified and schematic.

The retained bundled development library includes [Statue Of Unity India](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/statue-of-unity-india-86ec4a3582694b84af4017db0d396398) by [Chenzoss](https://sketchfab.com/Chenzoss), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The source pedestal and visitors were removed; the figure was recentered, normalized to 182 metres, given a bronze material and exported at two levels of detail. It is an artist's interpretation, not an authenticated scan. It is not among the nine playable landmarks in this edition. No endorsement by creators or monument authorities is implied.

The app icon is an original AI-assisted illustration. Screenshots render the submitted app’s actual game UI. Third-party licenses do not apply to Impossible City's proprietary app source.

Preview provenance: the three public images match the build 11 iPad listing assets, rendered in WebKit from the bundled game code using native edition styling. They are not physical-device captures or evidence of physical-device testing.
